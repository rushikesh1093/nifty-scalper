# Strategy performance report

**Bottom line:** none of the five enabled strategies has demonstrable positive
net expectancy. On the underlying, before any option cost, every baseline is
within a few hundredths of R of zero (Experiment A). After the measured cost of
expressing the trade through a bought option (~2.6 spot points plus decay), all
five are negative or indistinguishable from zero on 24 unseen months. Thirteen
pre-declared variants and six exit engines do not change that. No strategy meets
the paper-promotion gates (see [WALK_FORWARD_REPORT.md](WALK_FORWARD_REPORT.md)).
This is a research result, not a failure of the harness. The harness's job is to
say this when it is true.

stop). Rupee figures need a lot size and a premium path. They are only given where
recorded quotes exist (§6).

(2.6 spot pts + 1.5 pts per hour held, from recorded quotes and statutory charges);
HARSH (4 + 4/h). MEASURED is the primary model. The gross/net split per trade is
`r_gross`, `cost_r` and `r_net` in the CSVs. Spread and slippage are part of the
fixed 2.6 points (~0.8 spread/slippage + ~1.8 charges).

Baseline vs earlier work: the live entries re-run here reproduce the earlier
spot-simulation findings (FINDINGS.md §7-8: FCB +0.03R, ORB -0.01R, ST -0.04R,
EMA_RSI -0.13R, PIVOT +0.08R before costs on the 2023-25 years). The direction and
size match closely: this harness uses the live classes themselves, while that
work re-implemented them.

## 7. Reading the results

**Signal quality (Experiment A).** Directional hit at 30 minutes is 0.47-0.52 for every
baseline. +1R comes before the stop 38-55 % of the time. Median MFE and MAE to 15:15
are roughly mirror images (e.g. FCB +1.50R / -1.58R, PIVOT +2.25R / -2.13R). Each entry
is close to a coin flip on direction. The post-design pre-cost expectancies
(FCB +0.01, ORB +0.14, ST -0.02, EMA_RSI +0.04, PIVOT +0.19) all have bootstrap
intervals that include zero. Three of them were negative in the design year
(ORB -0.14, EMA_RSI -0.20, FCB -0.05), so the sign is not even stable.

**Execution (Experiment B and cost models).** Mean cost is 0.10-0.16R per trade for the
structural-stop strategies and 0.13R for SUPERTREND_EMA, whose median stop is only
22 spot points. With costs, SUPERTREND_EMA loses -0.147R per trade over 2,450 trades
(CI -0.176..-0.118, PF 0.61), the only result whose interval excludes zero: it is
**reliably negative**. Losses originate in signal quality (no edge). Option execution
turns a zero edge into a loss. See OPTION_EXECUTION_AUDIT §4.

**Experiment B on real quotes (table "Experiment B — recorded-quote option replay").** Only 46 baseline
trades fall on the 6 days with in-session recorded quotes. With ATM and 1-OTM contracts that makes 92
replay rows, of which 32 could be filled: 54 had no valid quote at entry and 6 none at exit. An unfilled row
is reported, never modelled. On the filled trades the option P&L follows the spot R
(ER +1.0R → +₹4,152; FCB -1.0R → -₹4,569), and charges plus spread are ₹56-109 per round
trip, consistent with the cost model. **This sample cannot support any profitability
statement.** It only confirms that the cost model's size is right.

**Per-strategy diagnosis.**

| Strategy | Main finding |
|---|---|
| FIRST_CANDLE_BREAKOUT | Wins come from TARGET and late SESSION_EXIT trades; 233 of 378 stop out. Breakouts against the 30-min EMA slope lose (-0.34R post-design), but the same split was the **opposite** sign in the design year (aligned -0.29R, counter 0.00R), so it is not a usable filter. STRONG (body ≥ 0.74, close in top 9 %) and RETEST variants do not help. |
| ORB_BREAKOUT | The only baseline near break-even after costs (+0.016R, n=155, CI -0.16..+0.20), but it was -0.27R in the design year. Displacement and volume filters look better post-design (+0.10R), yet both were ≤ 0 in the design year, have < 80 trades, and are 2 of 18 variants tried. Treat as noise until confirmed. VWAP alignment never removes a trade (a close above the OR high is always above VWAP). Retest is worse. |
| SUPERTREND_EMA | Structurally negative: ~0.73R target (8 premium points ≈ 16 spot points against a 22-point stop) with a 10-minute time stop, 5-minute average hold and 0.13R of cost per trade. Exit split: 1,302 targets, 1,272 stops, 936 time exits over 3 years. EMA 9/21 is worse (-0.175R). Pullback entries are slightly positive before costs (+0.04R) and the worst after (-0.21R). |
| EMA_RSI_PULLBACK_15M | -0.20R before costs in the design year, +0.04R after it: no stable signal. 3-min execution (ER_3M) enters earlier (median 22 min to 1R vs 52) but with tighter stops, so costs dominate (-0.13R). |
| PIVOT_BOUNCE | Best raw signal (+0.19R post-design before costs, +0.04R design), but a wide interval (-0.04..+0.41), and +0.03R after costs. PP/R1/S1 behave differently and not consistently across periods (S1 -0.11R in the design year, +0.12R after; R1 +0.40R then +0.19R). Sweep-and-reclaim entries do not improve it. |

**Exit engines (Part 8, identical entries).** No exit variant turns any strategy
positive after costs with an interval excluding zero. Wider, trend-following exits
(F: 5-min Supertrend trail; E: ATR chandelier) are the least bad for FCB, ST and
EMA_RSI because longer holds dilute the fixed cost. Even so, SUPERTREND_EMA stays at
-0.11R. EMA_RSI with the 5-min ST trail (+0.13R, CI -0.03..+0.30) is the largest
post-design improvement, but it was -0.05R before costs in the design year, so it is a
hypothesis, not a finding. Exits move losses around but cannot create an edge the
entry lacks.

**Ambiguity.** Candles touching both stop and target (counted as stops) are 0-3 per
variant over three years: OHLC resolution is not what drives these results.
